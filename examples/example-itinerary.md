# North Sea fortnight

Example only. Alex Quinn, Sam Patel, the dates, and every booking reference were invented for this run. A real session would not invent references. These are here so the map's `refs` field has something to show.

7 to 20 June 2027. Alex Quinn and Sam Patel. Pace: unhurried, with two days that have no plan at all.

## What was still open

The note already fixed the route, the modes (flight, train, overnight ferry, a hire car, and the long train south), the anniversary supper, the easy pace, and three optional ideas. One batch of questions covered the rest:

- Which Monday in June does the trip start?
- What names should the map show?
- How many nights in Bergen, Aarhus, and Copenhagen?
- Cabin on the ferry, or just seats?
- Where does the hire car get dropped?
- Are any other bookings already made, and should the references be printed?

The answers used for this example: start Monday 7 June 2027, print both names, three nights in Bergen, three in Aarhus, two in Copenhagen, three in Amsterdam, a cabin on the ferry, drop the car at Aarhus station, and print the made-up references below.

## Mon 7 Jun: Fly to Bergen

- Flight, Edinburgh 09:10 to Bergen 12:05. Example ticket NA7K2P.
- Three nights at Vågen House, a flat behind Bryggen. Example ref VH-20441. Check-in from 16:00.

## Tue 8 Jun: Bergen, unhurried

- No transfer. Bryggen, the fish market, and the Fløibanen.

## Wed 9 Jun: Train to Flåm

- Train, Bergen to Myrdal, then the Flåm railway. About two and a half hours.
- One night at the Fjord Room, by the station. Example ref FR-11830.

## Thu 10 Jun: Back, then the ferry

- Morning train back to Bergen.
- Overnight ferry, Bergen 20:30, Hirtshals about 08:00 on Friday. Cabin for two. Example ref NS-88421.

## Fri 11 Jun: Skagen and Aalborg

- Collect the car at the ferry terminal. Example ref KB-55210.
- Late morning in Skagen, where the two seas meet, then lunch.
- Drive south to Aalborg for the night.

## Sat 12 Jun: Drive to Aarhus

- About an hour and a quarter to Aarhus.
- Three nights at Latiner Rooms. Example ref LR-77820.
- Drop the car at the station by mid-afternoon. The rest of the stay is on foot.

## Sun 13 Jun: Aarhus

- ARoS and the old town.
- Optional, not in the plan: a loop south via Skanderborg to Moesgaard, then back to Aarhus. The map draws that ride home as one faint arc, not a reverse of each hop.

## Mon 14 Jun: Easy day

- Nothing planned. The Latin Quarter, coffee, an early night.

## Tue 15 Jun: Train to Copenhagen

- About three hours, no change worth planning around.
- Two nights in a Nyhavn attic flat. Example ref NH-33107.

## Wed 16 Jun: Copenhagen

- Nyhavn and a canal tour. Nothing else booked.
- Optional, not in the plan: Louisiana, up the coast.

## Thu 17 Jun: South by train

- Leave about 07:30. Change at Hamburg for lunch. Not an overnight there.
- Amsterdam by early evening.
- Three nights at Canal House on the Prinsengracht. Example ref CH-19044.

## Fri 18 Jun: Amsterdam

- The day is free until supper.
- Anniversary supper, a table for two on the Prinsengracht. Example ref TB-44190. This is the highlight on the map.

## Sat 19 Jun: The Jordaan

- Stay in the neighbourhood.
- Optional, not in the plan: Haarlem, a short train west.

## Sun 20 Jun: Fly home

- Train from the city to Schiphol in the late afternoon.
- Flight, Schiphol 18:40, Edinburgh 19:10. Same example ticket NA7K2P.

Edinburgh is the home gateway, so the "The trip" view leaves it off the map. Full route brings the two flights back.
